3 Open the AI tab

On any web page, press F12 (or right-click and choose Inspect), then open the AI tab. If you don't see it, click » in the DevTools tab bar.

Pages that were already open need a reload. Select an element in the Elements panel and ask about it, or just ask about the page. Nothing changes on the page until you approve it.
