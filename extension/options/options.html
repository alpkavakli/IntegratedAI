3 Start using it

Quick questions, on any page. Click the IntegratedAI icon in the toolbar, or press Alt+Shift+A. A small card opens on the page: ask about it, have it read or explain things, fix how it looks. Drag it anywhere, or against the side as a panel.
Opens a Wikipedia article with the card.
Everything, in DevTools. Press F12 (or right-click and choose Inspect) and open the AI tab (behind » if you don't see it). There it can also fill in forms and work through pages step by step, edit elements, save tasks to run again, and read the network log.
Pin the icon so it's always there: click the puzzle piece in the toolbar, then the pin next to IntegratedAI.

Pages that were already open need a reload. Nothing changes on a page until you approve it.
