Slow mornings, small rituals

By Ada Lindqvist · 6 min read

Most mornings begin before the light does. The kettle ticks as it warms, the window fogs at the edges, and for a few minutes nothing needs deciding.

I used to treat this hour as a runway: emails, headlines, a plan for the day. Lately I let it be shorter and quieter. One cup, one page of something written by someone else, one look at the weather.

None of it is productive in the usual sense. It is closer to tuning an instrument before playing it, a small act of attention that makes the rest of the day sound a little better.

If you try it, start smaller than you think. Five minutes is plenty. The point is not the ritual itself but noticing that you chose it.
